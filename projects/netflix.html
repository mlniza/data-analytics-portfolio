Background

What stories does Netflix data tell?

Netflix hosts thousands of titles across genres and countries, making it one of the richest datasets for understanding content strategy in the streaming industry. But what genres dominate globally? And how have documentary releases shifted over the past decade in the US?

This capstone project set out to answer those questions — exploring genre trends, country distributions, and the trajectory of documentaries on Netflix between 2012 and 2021 using a dataset of over 8,000 titles from Kaggle.

Business Questions

What we set out to answer

1
What is the most released genre on Netflix globally and specifically in the United States?
2
Out of the last 10 years (2012–2021), which year had the highest number of documentary releases in the US?
3
Who is the most prolific director and actor in the documentary genre on Netflix?

Insight

Key findings

359
Documentaries Lead Globally
Documentaries are the most released genre worldwide with 359 titles, followed by Stand-Up Comedy (334). However in the US, Stand-Up Comedy takes the top spot.
2017
Peak Year for Documentaries
2017 saw the highest number of documentary releases in the US with 54 titles — likely reflecting rising political awareness and the growth of streaming content.
54→3
Continuous Decline Post-2017
Documentary releases dropped consistently from 54 titles in 2017 to just 3 in 2021 — a significant shift in Netflix's US content strategy over four years.
382+
Samuel West Leads Actors
Samuel West is the most frequent actor in documentary genre films with 382+ appearances, while Vlad Yudin is the most prolific documentary director on the platform.
