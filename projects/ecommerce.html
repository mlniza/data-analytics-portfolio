Background

What drives e-commerce revenue?

E-commerce businesses generate massive amounts of transactional data every day, yet many decisions around discounting, promotions, and inventory are still made based on intuition rather than data. This project explores whether that data can tell a clearer story.

Using a dataset of 3,660 transactions across 2024, I analyzed sales performance by category, monthly revenue trends, discount effectiveness, and customer payment preferences — with the goal of turning raw numbers into actionable business recommendations.

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of all key metrics — filterable by category and date range. It covers revenue by category, monthly sales trend, payment method distribution, and discount vs revenue scatter analysis.

Insight & Recommendation

What the data revealed

👕
Clothing & Books Lead Revenue
These two categories consistently topped revenue across all months, making them the most reliable performers in the portfolio.
→ Prioritize stock availability and promotions for these categories
🏷️
High Discounts Hurt Revenue
Transactions with discounts above 30% generated lower revenue compared to those with 10–15% discounts — discounting more does not equal earning more.
→ Focus on moderate discounts (10–15%) to protect margins
💳
Payment Methods are Balanced
All 5 payment methods (UPI, COD, Net Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card) are distributed nearly equally — each around 20%, with less than 2% gap between them.
→ Keep all payment options available for maximum flexibility
📉
November Dip Needs Attention
Revenue peaked in April at 74K then declined significantly to 52K by November — a 30% drop that signals a need for year-end sales strategy.
→ Plan flash sales or bundling campaigns ahead of Q4
