### 1. Real-Time OEE Command Center
> Manufacturing Analytics · Internship Project

Built a live data pipeline and monitoring dashboard for 4 production machines — tracking availability, performance, quality, and downtime in real-time.

- **Tools:** Metabase, MySQL
- **Scope:** 4 machines (PU Cabinet N5, PU Door N5, Thermodoor N5, N5 Final)
- **Highlight:** Auto-refresh every 2 minutes, automated alert log for anomaly detection

---

### 2. E-Commerce Sales Analytics
> Sales Analytics · Personal Project

Analyzed job market and sales data to uncover revenue patterns, discount effectiveness, and customer payment preferences.

- **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query
- **Dataset:** Kaggle
- **Highlight:** 3,660 transactions across 8 product categories, Jan–Nov 2024

---

### 3. Netflix Genre Analysis
> Content Analytics · Kampus Merdeka x RevoU Capstone

Explored genre trends across 8,000+ Netflix titles with a spotlight on documentary releases in the US from 2012–2021.

- **Tools:** Looker Studio, Excel
- **Dataset:** Kaggle — Netflix Movies and TV Shows
- **Highlight:** Identified peak documentary year (2017) and consistent decline through 2021
- **Note:** Group project — personal contributions include data cleaning, dashboard development, and insight analysis
